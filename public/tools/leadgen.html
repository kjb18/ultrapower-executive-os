Step 5 — Make.com Setup Guide
Step-by-step instructions to connect your lead CSV to Make.com for automated drip sending. Free tier: 1,000 operations/month — enough for 10 leads/day.

🔧 Setup Steps

Export Leads to Google Sheets

Step 2 → Export CSV. Open Google Sheets → File → Import → upload the CSV. Name the sheet UP Lead Outreach.

Required columns: Name | Email | Company | Industry | Stage | Contacted | LastSent
Add a "Stage" column: New / Contacted / Follow-Up / Replied / Closed. Make.com reads and updates this column automatically.

Create a Make.com Account

Go to make.com → sign up free. Free tier: 1,000 ops/month. At 10 emails/day = ~300/month — well within the free limit.

Build the Scenario: Google Sheets → SMTP

Create new scenario in Make.com. Add these three modules:

Module 1: Google Sheets — Watch Rows Sheet: UP Lead Outreach Filter: Stage = "New" Module 2: Gmail / SMTP — Send Email To: {{Email}} Subject: [template from Step 3] Body: [email from Step 3] Module 3: Google Sheets — Update Row Row ID: {{Row ID from Module 1}} Stage: "Contacted" LastSent: {{now}}

Set Scheduling — Throttle to Prevent Spam

Clock icon → Set schedule → Every 2 hours, max 1 email per run. Mon–Fri only, 8AM–5PM PHT.

Schedule: Every 2 hours Max emails per run: 1 Active hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM PHT Days: Monday – Friday only
Never send cold emails on weekends or outside business hours. Open rates drop 40% and spam flags increase significantly.

Build Follow-Up Scenario (Day 5)

Duplicate scenario. Change filter: Stage = "Contacted" AND LastSent > 5 days ago. Load follow-up template. Update Stage to "Follow-Up 1".

Monitor Replies in Thunderbird

Replies arrive at [email protected]. A reply = warm lead → move to pipeline immediately via the Lead Tracker.

✅ Setup Checklist

⚠ Anti-Spam Rules

🚫 Never Break These
  • Never send more than 50 cold emails/day from a domain under 6 months old
  • Always include an unsubscribe line in every email
  • Never use ALL-CAPS or spam trigger words (FREE, URGENT, ACT NOW)
  • Warm up domain 28 days before cold outreach
  • Send from real name — Khalil Banares, not "Sales Team"
  • Only email publicly listed addresses — never purchased lists
✓ Best Practices
  • Keep initial email under 150 words
  • One clear ask per email — not multiple products
  • Personalize: first name + company name at minimum
  • Subject line: specific, industry-relevant, not salesy
  • Send Tuesday–Thursday 9AM–11AM PHT for best open rates
  • Stop after 3 unanswered touches
